Switch AI. Keep the conversation.

Open a chat on ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini in this tab to use Baton. If you just installed it, reload the tab.

  1. Open the chat you want to move and click Export.
  2. Open ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and click Import.
  3. Choose the file, then Insert. Press Send yourself.
Everything stays on your device. No servers, no tracking.
